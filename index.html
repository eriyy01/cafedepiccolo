Our story

Somewhere between a European cottage and a quiet Doon afternoon.

Café De Piccolo does not try too hard, yet stays with you. Every plate feels thoughtfully made and generously served. That is exactly why travellers keep coming back.

What really lingers is the feeling: a cozy, cottage like space, warm service, and conversations that stretch longer than planned.

“Some cafés you visit. Some feel like you have been there before.”
